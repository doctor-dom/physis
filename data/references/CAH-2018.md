# CAH screening — Pode-Shakked 2019 Table 1 percentile chart

Table 1 from Pode-Shakked et al. reports **17-OHP percentile values** stratified by birth weight alone, gestational age alone, or both. In PHYSIS this chart is used **alongside the Israeli NBS algorithm** to estimate where a measured 17-OHP falls relative to the screened population and to flag when published percentile thresholds are exceeded.

## Citation

Pode-Shakked N, Blau A, Pode-Shakked B, et al. Combined gestational age- and birth weight-adjusted cutoffs for newborn screening of congenital adrenal hyperplasia. *J Clin Endocrinol Metab*. 2019;104(8):3172-3180. doi:[10.1210/jc.2018-02468](https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2018-02468) (Table 1).

## Row selection

| Available inputs | Best-matching Table 1 row |
| --- | --- |
| Birth weight + gestational age | Combined BW and GA |
| Birth weight only | BW alone |
| Gestational age only | GA alone |

## Interpretation in PHYSIS

PHYSIS compares 17-OHP to **each published percentile** in the matched row (99.00th, 99.90th, 99.95th, etc.).

| Result | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 17-OHP exceeds one or more row percentile cutoffs | Further CAH workup suggested — interpret with Israeli NBS algorithm and clinical context |
| 17-OHP at or below all row percentile cutoffs | Estimated percentile position reported; no Table 1–driven workup flag |

## Notes

- Table 1 is **supplementary context**, not a standalone screening method in PHYSIS.
- Primary screening uses **Olgemöller 2003** (birth weight + sample age) and the **Israeli NBS algorithm** (Table 2).
- Values are assay- and program-specific; confirm with local newborn screening protocol.
