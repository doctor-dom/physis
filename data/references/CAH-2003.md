# CAH screening — birth weight and age-adjusted thresholds (2003)

Olgemöller et al. established multitiered 17-hydroxyprogesterone (17-OHP) recall thresholds adjusted for **birth weight** and **age at sample collection**, improving specificity of newborn CAH screening without loss of sensitivity.

## Citation

Olgemöller B, Roscher AA, Liebl B, Fingerhut R. Screening for congenital adrenal hyperplasia: adjustment of 17-hydroxyprogesterone cut-off values to both age and birth weight markedly improves the predictive value. *J Clin Endocrinol Metab*. 2003;88(12):5790-5794. doi:[10.1210/jc.2002-021732](https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2002-021732).

## Interpretation tiers

| Result | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Normal (≤ normal threshold) | CAH not suggested by this nomogram |
| Elevated (above normal, ≤ elevated threshold) | CAH possible — low-urgency recall / repeat sample |
| Markedly elevated (above elevated threshold) | CAH probable — high-urgency recall and endocrine referral |

## Notes

- Thresholds are expressed in **nmol/L serum** (filter-paper assays may differ; see assay-specific conversion in the source paper).
- Bavarian screening recommended sampling on **day 3** of life. The 2003 nomogram requires **birth weight and day of life at sample collection**; it is not shown unless both are entered.
